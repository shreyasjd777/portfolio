# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

React (Vite)

## Users

Recruiters, hiring managers, potential collaborators, and peers who land on the site from a resume, LinkedIn, GitHub, or a personal introduction. They are quickly assessing who this person is, what they've done, and whether to reach out.

## Product Purpose

A single-page personal portfolio site for a software engineer, serving as a durable professional presence to link from a resume, LinkedIn, or GitHub profile. It exists to give visitors a fast, credible read on the person's background, experience, and work — not to drive a hard conversion action.

## Positioning

Placeholder identity: a software engineer's personal site. No competing product to differentiate against — positioning here is about presenting the individual's own experience and projects credibly, not out-competing another portfolio.

## Operating Context

Single page, three content sections in order: About, Experience/Education, and Projects. A loading animation plays on initial page load and transitions into the landing page. All content (bio text, experience/education entries, project details, images) is placeholder for now and will be replaced with the real person's information and assets later.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Single-page scroll layout (no multi-page routing needed for v1).
- Sections: About, Experience/Education, Projects.
- Loading animation on first load, transitioning into the landing/hero view.
- Smooth scroll, hover, and click micro-animations throughout.
- Built with React (Vite), no backend — static content only at this stage.
- All copy, images, employer/school names, and project details are placeholder and clearly not real data yet.

## Brand Commitments

None yet — no real name, logo, or identity assets exist. Placeholder name/branding will be used until the person supplies real details.

## Evidence on Hand

None. No real bio, resume, work history, or project content has been provided. All content in the initial build is placeholder and must be replaced with real information later.

## Product Principles

- Fast, credible first impression: a visitor should understand who this person is and what they do within seconds of the loading animation finishing.
- Minimal and uncluttered: one page, three clear sections, no unnecessary complexity or noise competing with the content.
- Motion with restraint: scroll/hover/click animations and the glassmorphic surface should feel polished and purposeful, never gimmicky or distracting from the content.
- Durable and general-purpose: not tied to a single job search or client pitch — built to stay relevant as an ongoing professional presence.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No specific requirement established yet; standard web accessibility practices (keyboard navigation, reduced-motion support, sufficient contrast) should be followed by default.
